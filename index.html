About me

I am a PhD student in the ALGCo team at the LIRMM, Université de Montpellier under the supervision of Pascal Ochem and Matthieu Rosenfeld.

I am interested in combinatorics in general, with a particular enthusiasm for graph theory and extremal combinatorics.
